Privacy policy

The short version: your scores, answers, marks, name and settings stay on your phone. We never receive them. The free version shows ads from Google, and if you buy or subscribe to Baito Pro, the purchase is handled by Apple and checked through RevenueCat.

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Baito ("the app") is an app of unofficial Japanese practice tests and study lists for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. There is no account, no sign-up, and no server of ours that holds your progress.

Baito is unofficial practice. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Japanese-Language Proficiency Test (JLPT), the Japan Foundation or JEES.

What stays on your phone

Everything you do in Baito is stored locally on your device, in the app's private storage: your test attempts and answers, the kanji and words you star or mark as known, the name you ask Baito to call you (if any), your level, your reminder time and your other settings.

We cannot see this data. It is never uploaded to us, and it is not shared with the ad or purchase services described below. It leaves your phone only when you choose to back it up or send a report, as described next.

Your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup includes the app's data, under Apple's policies and your backup settings. If you delete Baito, the data on your phone is removed with it. We have no copy and cannot recover it for you, which is why the app offers a backup file.

Backup files you export yourself

Baito can export your progress, marks and a few settings (including your name, if you set one) to a backup file. This happens only when you choose to do it.

iOS hands the file to you through the standard share sheet, and you decide where it goes, for example Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop or an email to yourself. Once you send it somewhere, that destination's own privacy policy applies to it. The app does not upload the file anywhere, and we never receive it.

Importing a backup reads the file you pick and adds its progress to what is already on your phone.

Reports and feedback you email us

Report a problem (under questions, kanji and words) and Write feedback (in About) open your own mail app with an email already filled in. You see the whole message, including the address you send it from, and nothing is sent unless you tap Send. A report contains the item it is about (for example the question and its test), your note if you write one, and the app version.

If you send it, we receive your email address and the message. We use them only to fix content, improve the app and reply to you. To count repeat reports, we keep a scrambled code made from your address (a salted hash), not the address itself, alongside the report text. We do not add you to any mailing list. The app itself keeps no copy of the reports you send.

Daily reminder (optional)

If you turn on the daily reminder, iOS asks for permission to show notifications. The reminder is scheduled on your phone (a local notification). No notification token or other data is sent to us or anyone else.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version of Baito shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the browsing screens, a full-screen ad when a test starts, and, only if you choose to watch one, a rewarded ad that earns an extra test. Ads never appear while you answer a question.

To show and measure ads, Google's software in the app collects information about your device and how ads are used, such as:

  • your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (for example the country)
  • device identifiers: the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, and otherwise identifiers limited to this app or developer
  • which ads were shown and whether they were tapped
  • technical information such as the device model, iOS version, app performance and crash reports

None of your study progress, answers, name or settings are given to Google.

Consent and tracking are your choice. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Google's consent message asks first how your data may be used for ads; you can change your answer later in Settings > Privacy choices. Then iOS asks whether Baito may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you allow it, Google can use your advertising identifier to show ads that are more relevant to you. If you ask the app not to track, the advertising identifier is not shared and you still see ads, just less tailored ones. You can change this any time in the iPhone's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

The app asks Google to keep ad content within a rating suited to the app's App Store age rating.

How Google uses this information is described at policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

With Baito Pro, the app shows no ads and stops requesting them.

Baito Pro purchases and subscriptions (Apple and RevenueCat)

Baito Pro is available as a yearly or monthly subscription or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details or your Apple Account.

To confirm and restore Baito Pro, the app uses a service called RevenueCat. When you open the Baito Pro screen, buy, subscribe or tap Restore purchase, and each time the app starts while you have a subscription (to check whether it has renewed), the app sends RevenueCat:

  • your purchase history for this app (which product was bought or subscribed to, when, and the App Store receipt that proves it)
  • a random, anonymous ID that the app creates for this purpose, so the purchase can be recognised again, for example after reinstalling
  • basic technical information such as the app version, iOS version and the country or region of your App Store account

The anonymous ID is not your name, email or Apple Account, and we do not link it to you. RevenueCat does not receive your study progress or settings. If you never open the Baito Pro screen, buy, subscribe or restore, the app does not contact RevenueCat at all.

RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your iPhone's Settings, under your name > Subscriptions.

Other information

The App Store itself may collect information about downloads, purchases and crashes under Apple's own policies. That is between you and Apple. Baito has no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own.

Permissions

Baito asks for a permission only when it needs it: the tracking question described above, notifications if you turn on the daily reminder, and the share sheet or file picker when you export or import a backup. It does not request contacts, location services, camera, photos, microphone or Health data.

Deleting your data

  • On your phone: delete the app. Backup files you exported stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.
  • Emails you sent us: email us and we will delete your messages and any report records made from them.
  • Advertising: you can reset or limit your advertising identifier and tracking in the iPhone's Settings > Privacy & Security. Google's data choices are described in its privacy policy.
  • Purchase records: because the RevenueCat ID is anonymous, we cannot look up your record from your name or email. If you want it deleted, email us and we will help as far as we can.

Children

Baito is made for teenagers and adults learning Japanese and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Your progress is held in your device's app-private storage and is protected by your device passcode, Face ID or Touch ID and by iOS's own file protection. It is never sent to a server of ours, so there is nothing of ours holding it that could be breached. The trade-off is that we cannot restore it for you if you lose your phone, so please keep a backup.
